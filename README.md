> Building at the intersection of technology, business, design, and human behavior.

I'm an entrepreneur and multidisciplinary builder based in Istanbul, Türkiye. I think in systems, not isolated solutions: a website is part of a customer journey, a product is part of a business model, and a business is a collection of decisions, processes, incentives, and people.

My work is shaped by a simple idea: the most valuable thing you can build is not always a product. Sometimes, it is the system that makes everything else work.

## How I Build

01 — **Understand**  
Explore the problem, question assumptions, investigate the market, and identify the people affected.

02 — **Structure**  
Define the opportunity, establish priorities, map relationships, and design the underlying system.

03 — **Build**  
Turn concepts into working products, workflows, and experiences. Favor useful implementations over unnecessary sophistication.

04 — **Validate**  
Observe actual behavior, collect feedback, measure outcomes, and challenge initial expectations.

05 — **Evolve**  
Improve what works, remove what does not, and reconsider the architecture when evidence demands it.
